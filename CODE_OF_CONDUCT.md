# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, and
personal attacks are not welcome here — full stop. This is a small project; we'd like it to
stay a pleasant place to build something good.

## The standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short, we pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that
would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing
the project in public.

Report violations privately to the maintainer at the address in the repo profile. Reports
will be reviewed and handled discreetly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions
and comments that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone
for behavior they deem inappropriate.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
