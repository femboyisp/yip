# Funding yip

yip is free software (AGPL-3.0-or-later), pre-1.0, and independent. It isn't for sale and
it isn't chasing anyone's approval — but building it takes real time, and if yip is useful to
you, support keeps that time flowing. Every avenue below is optional and no-strings. Fund it
however suits you; the code stays open either way.

## Other ways to support

- **Recurring** — [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/femboyisp).
- **Contribute** — code, review, testing, docs, or a real-world deployment report. See
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Collaborate** — NGOs and mesh operators who need yip's properties, privacy-VPN projects
  interested in upstreaming FEC/anti-DPI work, and researchers are all welcome to reach out.
- **Grants** — internet-freedom funders (NLnet, Mozilla, the Zcash Foundation, Mullvad's OSS
  support) are one avenue, on our terms and our timeline, not a dependency.

## What support buys

Development is milestone-based and lands in the open (see [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md)):
multi-core throughput, traffic-analysis defense, control-plane hardening, and a post-quantum
handshake. No paywalls, no "enterprise edition" — the AGPL guarantees it stays that way.

**Contact:** open an issue, or reach the maintainer at the address in the repo profile.
